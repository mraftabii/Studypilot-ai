# 📚 StudyPilot AI

**Your goals. Your plan. Your progress.**

StudyPilot AI is a student-focused study planner designed to help students organize their studies, manage tasks, track exam deadlines, and stay motivated.

## ✨ Features

- 🎓 **Exam Mode:** Add exams with subjects, syllabus topics, dates, and times.
- ✅ **Task Mode:** Create tasks with subjects, estimated durations, and deadlines.
- ⏳ **Live Countdown:** Track the time remaining until exams and task deadlines.
- 📋 **All Tasks & Exams:** View your tasks and exams together on the home page.
- 💾 **Local Storage:** Save your tasks and exams in your browser.
- 💡 **Daily Motivation:** Stay motivated with rotating motivational quotes.
- 📱 **Responsive Design:** Use the planner on desktop and mobile screens.
- ✔️ **Progress Tracking:** Mark tasks and exams as completed or delete them when needed.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- Browser Local Storage

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open the StudyPilot AI website.
2. Choose **Exam Mode** to add an exam or **Task Mode** to create a task.
3. Enter the required details, including dates, times, and deadlines.
4. View your tasks and exams on the home page.
5. Track countdowns and mark items as completed.
